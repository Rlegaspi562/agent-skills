## Format DNA (steal this)

1. **Half-and-half stage** — fixed horizontal split; graphics never fight the face.
2. **Black-pill / black-box captions** — high contrast, short phrase chunks, center on the split line or over torso.
3. **Flat tech diagrams** — off-white board, black strokes, one accent color (red peak / green OK / red fail).
4. **Icon callouts** — product logos + simple badges instead of wall of text.
5. Talking-head energy stays (mic in hand, gestures) while the top panel does the "show".
