# Preset: CapCut-style center captions

**id:** `capcut-center-captions`

## When to use
Body of talking-head shorts after the hook. Matches speech cadence without
stealing the kinetic hook energy.

## Look
- Clean sans, medium/regular weight, **sentence case**, white.
- Subtle dark drop shadow or thin stroke for rooftop / busy BG legibility.
- Centered mid-frame (not burn-in lower-third).

Closest fonts: **Montserrat Medium**, **Inter SemiBold**, **Helvetica Neue**.

## Motion
- Word-by-word or 2–3 word chunks appear in sequence.
- Each chunk: instant appear or micro scale-up (~95%→100%).
- Clear all text at natural pauses / new thought.
- No bounce (save bounce for hook preset).

## SFX
Usually **silent** under VO once music bed is in; avoid pop spam on every word.

## DaVinci Resolve Studio
1. Transcribe VO (or paste script) into Resolve Subtitles, then convert
   preferred words to **Text+** on V2 for style control — or use a caption
   macro / Fusion template that advances by word.
2. Text+: Tracking tight; Shadow soft; center aligned.
3. Cut caption clips to VO word boundaries; keep 1–2 frames of lead if needed.
4. Export a **Power Bin** preset named `Rumil/CapCut Center Captions` once
   locked.
