# Preset: SFX text pop + whoosh

**id:** `sfx-text-pop-whoosh`

## When to use
Hook kinetic type and fast montage; back off during long VO captions.

## Palette
- **Pop/click:** short, pitched up slightly per word (avoid same sample every
  hit — vary pitch ±1–2 semitones).
- **Whoosh:** low/mid whoosh on script reveal, flash, or B-roll smash.
- **Shutter:** only with `white-flash-shutter`.
- **Music:** lo-fi / chill hip-hop bed under VO after hook.

## Fairlight
1. Place pops on a dedicated SFX track; snap to Text+ in-points.
2. Sidechain or manually duck music ~1–2 dB under VO, not under pops.
3. Keep SFX peak under VO intelligibility.
