# Preset: Kinetic hook dual-font

**id:** `kinetic-hook-dual-font`

## When to use
Opening 1–3 seconds of a talking-head short when you want a punchy claim with
one emotional or noun accent (e.g. "Followers", "First").

## Look
- **Primary:** Heavy / black extended sans, ALL CAPS, white `#FFFFFF`, soft
  feathered black drop shadow (not a hard CapCut stroke).
- **Accent word:** Elegant script / cursive, white, often larger than the sans
  line; sits on the key noun.
- **Position:** Center or upper-center, stacked lines.

Closest free fonts to try: **Montserrat Black** or **Anton** (sans); **Great
Vibes** or **Allura** (script). Prefer a true extended black if licensed
(Monument Extended–like).

## Motion
1. Each sans word/chunk: scale pop ~80% → ~105% → 100% over ~8–12 frames.
2. Accent script: write-on or left-to-right wipe (not the same pop).
3. Stagger words to VO emphasis; one beat per pop.

## SFX
Pair with `sfx-text-pop-whoosh` (short pitched pop per sans hit; soft whoosh on
script reveal).

## DaVinci Resolve Studio
1. **Edit page → Titles → Text+** (or Fusion Text) for each word as its own
   clip on V2+.
2. Animate **Zoom X/Y** with keyframes; use spline overshoot for bounce.
3. Soft shadow: Text+ Shadow with low opacity and larger softness (avoid 100%
   hard outline unless body captions need it).
4. Script layer: separate Text+; animate **Write-on** (Character Level) or a
   horizontal wipe mask.
5. Optional: nest hook stack; add `white-flash-shutter` on the cut out of hook.
