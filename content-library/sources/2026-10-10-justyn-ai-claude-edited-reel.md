## Why it works

1. Hook is a bold negative claim ("I didn't edit a single second") with the reveal ("Claude did.") inside 3s.
2. Self-proof: the format is the evidence, so viewers rewatch to inspect the edits.
3. Demonstrate as you say it: "make it moody" and the frame goes moody right then.
4. Shows a real artifact (storyboard with beats, emphasis, sound) which reads as a credible system, not a gimmick.
5. Keyword comment CTA with a concrete deliverable (walkthrough), which explains comments 2.3x likes. Guess: the comment volume also feeds distribution.
